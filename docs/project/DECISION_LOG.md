## Rules

- **Append-only.** Reverse a decision by adding a row that cites the earlier ID.
- **One row per decision.** Detailed alternatives and consequences belong in an ADR.
- **Human accountability.** Record a real named owner once established; never infer one from a filesystem username or substitute an AI author.
- **Status stays explicit.** Prices, store obligations, identity/retention choices, capacity targets and the shared-engine/mobile designs are not approved merely because their proposals are documented. Licensing and unsupported-platform exclusions have no decision here; template examples are not project policy.
